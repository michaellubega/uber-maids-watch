The process

A clear path from first request to settled placement

Every step is handled by the office and recorded on your account. You can follow progress in the portal instead of chasing updates by phone.

Verify

Identity, references and background checks are completed before a worker is presented to any family.

Office-controlled

Train

Workers go through household practice and conduct orientation so expectations are clear on both sides.

Prepared for the role

Match

We compare your household needs, schedule and location with available, verified workers.

Reviewed by staff

Place

The placement is confirmed in writing, with start date, terms and guarantee period recorded.

Documented

Track & support

Follow-ups are scheduled after placement so the office can check how the household and worker are settling in, and record any concern early.

Scheduled check-ins

Replace when eligible

If something goes wrong, you can raise a replacement request in the portal. Eligibility, timing and any cost are decided by the office against the terms on your placement — the portal shows the decision and its reason.

Reviewed by a manager
